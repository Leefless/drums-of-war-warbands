The rules engine and map are solid and the server survived about 81,000 junk messages and 640 soak battles without a crash or a leaked seat token. What holds the score down is the battle experience at its edges: a player knocked out or removed stares at a black board, a pause can wait on someone who cannot see the attack, and units can end a stance turn hanging off the table and then die for it. The test suite is the other weak spot: of 102 planted engine bugs it caught 33, including none that would leak a seat token through the pending choice, the winner or bought terrain.
