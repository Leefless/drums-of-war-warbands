Completed this session

Terrain with tactical effects

  • Hill: +1 weapon range, sees farther and over forests, +1 defence vs attackers below. Climbing costs strain; never hidden.
  • Forest: cover, blocks sight, hides units. Strain to enter, -1 die firing out.
  • Ruins: cover and blocks sight; rubble damage on entry.
  • Lava: burns and stops movement; heat haze protects units behind it. Calamity is immune and draws power.
  • Marsh: stops movement, -1 defence inside; mist protects units behind it.
  • Rocks: impassable and fully block sight.
  • Rift: unchanged risk and reward.

Fog of war and stealth

  • Each player sees only enemies their units can spot: 3 range bands, 4 from a hill, line of sight required.
  • Stealthy units and units in forests are only spotted from range 1.
  • Ambush: attacking from out of sight adds a die. Firing reveals you for the round.
  • Unseen units are stripped from your screen, your log and every data field the server sends, not just hidden visually.
  • The board darkens outside your units' sight; hovering terrain explains its effects.

Better terrain art

  • Three painted battlefields (meadow, autumn moor, ash scar), one chosen per battle.
  • Six painted variants of every terrain kind (42 sprites) with cast shadows, auto-trimmed to their rules footprint.
  • Scattered ground decor and warm directional light.
  • All generated in your ChatGPT chat and delivered to the game through a localhost-only handoff, so no manual file moves were needed while you were away.

Decisions I made for you to review

You asked me to go with my recommendations. These are the calls that change how the game plays or works; each is easy to reverse.

  • Watchers see every unit. Watch only screens ignore fog of war, so they work as a table TV. A seated player could open one to peek; that is a table-trust call.
  • Concord gained a new rule, Discipline: +1 defence die while another friendly unit is at range 1. This was the cleanest fix for the weakest army and fits its "nobody fights alone" identity.
  • Champions cost 15% more than their computed value; with the other price changes, champion armies dropped from about 60% to 56% wins.
  • Cost formula retuned: Steel, Channel, Warcry and Inspire priced up; Master, Thrall, Rally and Command priced down.
  • Terrain triggers on entering, not every round a unit stands in it. Standing effects (cover, high ground) still apply continuously.
  • Masters can Command twice (two different units), matching the Puppet Strings description.
  • At the round limit, only warbands still on the field can win. An eliminated player with high VP no longer takes the game.
  • Duplicate units get numbered names ("Ork Boyz (2)"), which also makes fog-of-war log scrubbing exact.
  • Deployment zones are 180 deep (were 110) so a full army of large bases fits in two ranks; terrain keeps clear of every zone.
  • Two Umbral units lost Rally, which they could never use (Deathless units never gain strain), and got Guard and Steel instead.
  • Two Calamity portrait sheets used plainer descriptions after ChatGPT's violence filter refused the originals; the art matches the units.
